**Medical Records Template**

-   Date

-   Location of visit (ED, CCC intake, family medicine, etc.)

-   Name

-   Age

-   Reason for visit: Typically found in chief complaint or chief
    complaints or visit diagnosis or visit diagnoses

-   Pull out relevant medications: Typically found in discharge
    medication list or active at the end of visit or medication list

    -   Medication

    -   Start date

    -   Instructions

    -   Any medications discontinued or stopped in visit

        -   Medication

        -   Date

        -   Instructions

    -   Any medications not being taken by patient or patient not
        compliant with

        -   Medication

        -   Date

        -   Instructions

-   Hospital course summary/HPI or subjective or interval history

-   Clinical notes OR progress notes

-   Current facility administered medications

    -   Opioids (Percocet or oxycodone-acetaminophen, Vicodin or
        hydrocodone-acetaminophen, Oxycodone, Tramadol, Codeine,
        Morphine, Methadone, Demerol, Tylenol-Codeine #3) administered

-   Social history

    -   Substance use history

        -   Alcohol use

        -   Drug use

            -   Types

        -   Amount

        -   Frequency of use

    -   Mental health history

        -   GAD 7

        -   PHQ-9 Depression Screen

    -   Family History

    -   Marital status

    -   Smoking status

        -   Years

    -   Sexual activity

    -   Occupational history

    -   Any mention of homeless or homelessness or living in a shelter
        or living on the street(s)

-   Any drugs mentioned

-   PMH (past medical history)

    -   Diagnosis or diagnoses

    -   Date

-   Results/Labs

    -   Only include Toxicology Screen and Pain management panel

-   Referrals or follow-up plan

-   Any mention of MAT or MOUD or Suboxone or Buprenorphine or SUD
    treatment or residential treatment or detox

    -   Specific name of facility if patient transferred or discharged
        there

-   Any mention of Thrive or peer support
